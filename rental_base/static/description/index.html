Rental Base

Base Module for Rental Management

This module provides a new menu for rental management. It is based on the sale_rental module of this repository.

Configuration

Only the members of the group ‘See Rental Menu’ see the Rentals menu. The superuser and the administrator get the group when the module is installed. To give it to another user:

  • Go to Settings > Users & Companies > Users.
  • Open the user.
  • In the tab ‘Access Rights’, section ‘Other’, set ‘Rental’ to ‘See Rental Menu’.
  • Save.

When the module is updated to version 17.0.1.1.0 from an earlier version, every active user with access to Sales gets the group ‘See Rental Menu’.

The field ‘Rental Time Unit’ of the order lines, needed to rent by week, month or hour, is only visible with the units of measure:

  • Go to Sales > Configuration > Settings.
  • In the block ‘Product Catalog’, activate ‘Units of Measure’.
  • Save.

Usage

The Rentals menu is visible to the members of the group ‘See Rental Menu’ (see the configuration section).

Create a rentable product and its rental service:

  • Go to Rentals > Configuration > Settings.
  • Activate the checkbox ‘Variants’.
  • Go to Rentals > Products > Products.
  • Create a new storable product.
  • Activate the checkbox ‘Can be Rented’.
  • Go to the tab ‘Rental’ and click ‘Create Rental Service’.
  • Set the ‘Rental Service Name’ and the ‘Rental Price per Day’.

Create a rental order:

  • Go to Rentals > Customers > Quotations > Rental Quotations.
  • Create a new order and choose the ‘Order Type’ ‘Rental Order’.
  • Add the rental service as an order line. In the group ‘Rental Settings’ of the line, the option ‘Rental’ of ‘Order Type’ is selected; ‘Normal’ is for the lines that are not a rental, and confirming the order creates no rental for them.
  • Set the ‘Rental Quantity’ to rent out one or several storable rentable products.
  • Choose the ‘Start Date’ and the ‘End Date’. The group ‘Rental Times’ shows the ‘Number of Days’ and the ‘Ordered Rental Time’.
  • Confirm the order.
  • Check out the two deliveries, one for outgoing and one for incoming delivery.

Rent by day, week, month or hour:

  • In the order line of a rental service, choose the ‘Rental Time Unit’: Days, Week(s), Month(s) or Hours.
  • The ‘Ordered Rental Time’ is the ‘Rental Quantity’ multiplied by the number of time units from the start date to the end date, both included:
    • Days: one per day.
    • Week(s): every started week counts as a whole week.
    • Month(s): the number of days divided by 30.4167, the average length of a month, rounded to the nearest whole month.
    • Hours: for each day, the ‘Average Hour per Day’ of the ‘Default Working Hours’ of the company of the order, or 8 when there is none.

For example, renting 2 products from January 1 to January 15, 2021 in Week(s) counts 3 weeks, so the ‘Ordered Rental Time’ is 6. In Month(s), the same 15 days are less than half a month and count 0 months, January 1 to January 31, 2021 counts 1, and January 1 to March 1, 2021 (60 days) counts 2.

Find rental orders and sales orders:

  • In the lists of quotations and orders, the search filter ‘Rental Order’ shows the orders whose ‘Order Type’ is ‘Rental Order’, and ‘Normal Order’ shows all the others.
  • Sales > Orders > Quotations and Sales > Orders > Orders open with the filter ‘Normal Order’ active.
  • Rentals > Customers > Quotations > Rental Quotations and Rentals > Customers > Orders > Rental Orders open with the filter ‘Rental Order’ active.
  • Remove the filter to see both kinds of orders.

See also the usage section of the sale_rental module.
