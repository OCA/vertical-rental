In the *Products* menu, on the form view of a storable product or
consumable, in the *Sales* tab, there is a button *Create Rental
Service* which starts a wizard to generate the corresponding rental
service.

Rental is not enabled on any warehouse by default: you have to select
the warehouses you want to rent from. In the menu *Inventory \>
Configuration \> Warehouse Management \> Warehouses*, on the form view
of the warehouse, in the *Warehouse Configuration* tab, check the option
*Rental Allowed*. This creates two additional stock locations, visible
in the *Technical Information* tab: *Rental In* (stock of products to
rent) and *Rental Out* (products currently rented).
